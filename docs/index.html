SW: DBG
Campaign Companion

Track campaigns, factions and deck changes, or quickly set up a game with random faction assignments.

Designed as a companion for players of Star Wars: Deckbuilding Game.

Two ways to organize your game

Choose the mode that matches the game you're about to play.

Mode 01

Single Game

Set up a game for 2–4 players and let the app handle faction assignment.

  • Choose 2–4 players.
  • Select the factions to use.
  • Randomly assign factions to players.
  • Use player names instead of player numbers.
Mode 02

Campaign Companion

Keep the information from your four-game campaign in one place so you know how to prepare the next game.

  • Set up a two-player campaign.
  • Select factions and reinforcements.
  • Record cards removed from decks.
  • Record cards added to decks.
  • Save multiple campaigns.
  • Use custom player names.

Privacy

SW: DBG – Campaign Companion does not require an account and does not collect or transmit personal data. Campaign information is stored locally on your device.
