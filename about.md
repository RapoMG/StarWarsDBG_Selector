[font=fonts/StarJedi-DGRW.ttf] Star Wars DBG Campaign Companion[/font]

Unofficial companion app for [i]Star Wars: The Deckbuilding Game[/i].

This app provides [b]two main features[/b]:

[b]Faction Selector[/b]
Randomly assigns factions, starting player, and (optionally) the neutral deck for 2–4 players.

[b]Campaign Companion[/b]
Track campaign progress by managing decks, destroyed bases, Force levels, and reinforcement decks.
The app validates deck sizes and warns about rule deviations before the next game.

In the app’s settings, you’ll find the option to change player names and starting cards for each faction.
